# IndividualProject
Personalized Learning System for Students with ADHD

This project aims to develop a personalized learning platform for students with ADHD to enhance focus, engagement, and academic performance.

OBJECTIVES
To create a system that personalizes learning content based on individual ADHD profiles.
To provide adaptive learning pathways to enhance their engagement and academic performance.
To integrate accessibility tools that are isolated solutions in one platform
To adjust content delivery to ensure continuous support as the student progresses through educational stages.
To design an intuitive interface that supports ease of use for ADHD students and their educators.
To raise the neuro-awareness of ADHD students as well as their educators.
